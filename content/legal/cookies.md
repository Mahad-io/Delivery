## The rule we follow

Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) we may only set a cookie without your consent if it is strictly necessary to provide a service you have asked for. Everything else needs your consent first, and refusing must be as easy as accepting.

So: the banner has an equally prominent "Reject" and "Accept" button, refusing is one click, and nothing optional is set until you choose. The banner does not block the site — you can ignore it and keep reading. We do not ask again for six months unless the policy version changes.

## What we do not use

No Google Analytics. No advertising or retargeting pixels. No social media buttons or embeds. No session-recording or heatmap tools. No fingerprinting. No third-party fonts, scripts or images — every asset is served from this domain, which is also why the site loads quickly. No cross-site tracking of any kind, and nothing on this site talks to another domain.

There is no advertising on {{SITE_NAME}} and no advertiser can pay to influence what appears here.

## Changing your mind

Go to [{{BASE_URL}}/cookies]({{BASE_URL}}/cookies) at any time. Turning something off takes effect immediately and clears the associated storage. If you have an account, your choice is saved against it so it follows you between devices.

Every change you make is recorded in your consent history, which you can see in your data export.

## Browser controls

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and set your browser to tell you when a site tries to set one. Blocking the strictly necessary cookie will prevent sign-in and form submission. Guidance for each major browser is on its own support site, and the ICO publishes plain-English guidance on cookies at ico.org.uk.
